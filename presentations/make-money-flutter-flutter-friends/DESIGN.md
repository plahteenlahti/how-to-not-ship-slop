# Design System: Make money with your Flutter app — Flutter & Friends

## Visual direction

Keep the editorial conference-deck structure from the Droidcon presentation:
large off-axis claims, one dominant region per slide, clear evidence objects,
restrained motion, and stage-readable type. Use the Flutter & Friends palette
instead of the Droidcon coral system.

## Palette

- Paper: `#F7F6F0`
- Ink: `#171B25`
- Flutter blue: `#3F8DEF`
- Deep blue: `#246DCC`
- Lavender: `#9C87F5`
- Blush: `#FFD0CD`
- Mist: `#E7F2FB`

## Typography

- Display and body: Outfit
- Code and metadata: JetBrains Mono
- Keep headings short and large enough for the back of the room.

## Editing

Every slide is a clearly labeled `<section>` in `index.html`. Text is written
directly in the HTML instead of being generated from a JavaScript content
array. Press `E` while presenting to edit visible slide text in the browser;
those quick edits are stored in that browser's local storage.
